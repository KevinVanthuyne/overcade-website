One of the most iconic and influential arcade games of all time. Space Invaders is widely regarded as one of the
founding pillars of the modern video game industry.

In this game you shoot down waves of alien invaders as they slowly move towards you. A laser cannon lets you take out
the creatures and score points. Once every alien is defeated you move on to the next level, which gets harder every
time.

Space Invaders was the first game to introduce a high score system, motivating players to keep beating their own
results. Its success sparked a worldwide arcade boom and the start of the golden age of arcade games. Legend has it
that Japan even ran short of Yen coins because everyone was using them to play Space Invaders.

The game was developed by Tomohiro Nishikado and released by Taito in 1978. By his own account the creator of Space
Invaders was never a good player and admitted he rarely got past the first level.
