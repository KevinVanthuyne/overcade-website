An arcade icon with a twist! Centipede is a unique mix of strategy and fast reflexes, controlled with a trackball.

In this game you control a small cannon at the bottom of the screen, called the Bug Blaster. The goal is to destroy a
centipede that winds its way down through a field of mushrooms. Every time you hit a segment the centipede splits and
moves faster. Meanwhile spiders, fleas and scorpions make things even harder.

Centipede is praised for its simple but addictive gameplay. The unique trackball control makes aiming the cannon smooth
and precise. After Ms. Pac-Man and Pac-Man, Centipede was, together with Donkey Kong, the highest-earning game of 1982.
It was also one of the first arcade games to become popular with male and female players alike.

The game was developed by Atari and released in 1980. Ed Logg and Dona Bailey, one of the few female developers at the
time, were behind this iconic game. Centipede was a huge success and later received several sequels and remakes.
