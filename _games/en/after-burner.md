An explosive arcade experience full of speed and spectacle. After Burner is pure adrenaline and leaves nobody untouched.

In After Burner you climb into the cockpit of an F-14 fighter jet and take on waves of enemy aircraft. Missiles and
machine guns blast everything out of the sky while the landscape races past, but watch out for the missiles heading
your way!

After Burner is an impressive all-round experience with a vibrating flight stick, a throttle lever and flashing lights
whenever you have an enemy in your sights. Scaling and rotating sprites created a convincing 3D illusion long before
real 3D graphics were possible.

The game was developed and released by Sega in 1987, a year after the hit film Top Gun came out, which is where After
Burner drew most of its inspiration from.
