Quite possibly the ultimate arcade classic. Pac-Man is widely considered one of the most influential video games ever
made and is one of the most recognisable icons in pop culture.

In this game you control Pac-Man and have to eat every dot in the maze without being caught by one of the four coloured
ghosts. But eat one of the big "Power Pellets" and the roles are reversed, letting Pac-Man eat the ghosts!

Pac-Man was one of the first arcade games to break away from the space shooters that were popular at the time, such as
Space Invaders and Galaxian. It was the first game built around a character and the first to feature cutscenes.
All of that also made it a hit with female players.

The game was developed by Namco and licensed for distribution in the United States by Midway in 1980. An estimated
400,000 cabinets were built worldwide.
