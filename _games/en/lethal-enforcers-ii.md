The ultimate shooting experience in the Wild West! Lethal Enforcers 2 brings the revolver duel to the arcade.

In this classic rail shooter you take on the role of a sheriff who has to keep the peace in a town full of criminals.
From banks packed with bandits to saloons full of trigger-happy desperados, you shoot your way through dangerous
situations. Aim quickly and accurately to restore order, but avoid the innocent bystanders!

Lethal Enforcers 2 stands out for its use of digital photography, which made the graphics realistic for its time. This
sequel builds on the success of the original Lethal Enforcers, but drops players into a new Wild West setting full of
dusty cowboys and intense gunfights. The game also supports two players, which makes playing together even better.

The game was developed and released by Konami in 1994. It also came out on several home consoles with a light gun
accessory, which grew its popularity further.
