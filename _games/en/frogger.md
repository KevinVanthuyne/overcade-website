Frogger is a timeless classic from the golden age of arcade games. It is an iconic game that is still loved by players
of all ages.

In this game you guide a frog across a busy road and a treacherous river to reach the other side. Once all five frogs
are safely across, you move on to the next level. Dodge the cars and crocodiles, and avoid the diving turtles. Pick up
the female frog along the way or eat the flies for extra points.

Frogger earned the dubious honour of being the arcade game with the most ways to die. With no fewer than nine ways to
meet your end, the game is quite the challenge. Frogger also appeared in the popular TV series Seinfeld, where an entire
episode was dedicated to the game.

The game was developed by Konami and published by Sega in 1981. In the US the arcade reached the market through
Sega/Gremlin. Its success led to countless sequels and adaptations on all kinds of platforms.
