Dive into the world of the famous superhero Spider-Man with smooth gameplay and clear objectives. An ideal machine for
beginners, while still being a challenge for experienced players to reach the end game.

Take on 4 of Spider-Man's best known enemies: Green Goblin, Doc Ock, Sandman and Venom, and save New York City from
their evil plans!

Fun features include the mechanical Doc Ock magnet that grabs the ball, Sandman targets that sink into the playfield,
and the Green Goblin that shakes when he is hit. The game combines fast action with recognisable quotes, sound effects
and imagery from the original Spider-Man films by Sam Raimi.

Spider-Man was released in 2007 by Stern Pinball and remains one of their most popular machines from the 2000s. Steve
Ritchie, known as "The Master of Flow", designed the playfield, and Lyman Sheets, the legendary programmer, worked out
the rules.
