Ready to take on Skynet? Save humanity with an arcade that comes straight out of the famous Terminator action film.
Terminator 2: Judgment Day is explosive sci-fi shooting action in its purest form.

Armed with a heavy machine gun you blast apart everything Skynet throws at you: flying drones, Terminators and of
course the infamous T-1000. The mission is simple: protect the leader of the resistance and stop the downfall of
humanity.

What makes this arcade so impressive is the real recoil of the guns, which makes you feel like you are right in the
middle of the action. The graphics were stunning for their time too: digitised versions of the actors made the game
look genuinely realistic. Arnold Schwarzenegger even reprised his role as the Terminator for the arcade.

The arcade was released in 1991 by Midway in collaboration with the makers of the film, right after the blockbuster
came out.
