A modern pinball machine with a knockout theme: the famous Avengers superheroes from the comics, series and films of
the Marvel universe.

Collect the 6 Infinity Stones by defeating Thanos' henchmen before Thanos gets hold of the stones himself. Take on the
Black Order and Thanos in this action-packed machine.

Avengers is known for its challenging gameplay and deep rule set. The Pro version has a number of unique features, such
as the spinning Dr. Strange disc that opens portals and starts quests, and the Avengers Tower that can hold the ball in
mid-air to start the Iron Man Multiball.

The machine was released in 2020 by Stern Pinball, currently the largest pinball manufacturer in the world. It was
designed by Keith Elwin, one of the best pinball players in the world and one of the most successful and best-loved
modern designers.
